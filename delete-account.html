Delete Your Account & Data

Last updated: 5 August 2026

Eco Family Flow lets you permanently delete your account and all data associated with it. There are two ways to do this.

Option 1: Delete it yourself in the App (fastest)

  1. Open Eco Family Flow and sign in.
  2. Go to the Account tab.
  3. Tap 🗑️ Delete Account & Data and confirm.

This immediately and permanently removes:

  • All of your logged entries (feeds, nappies, medications, hospital notes, appointments)
  • Your stash balance for every child profile
  • Your membership in any shared family (or the family itself, if you created it)
  • Your Google sign-in record for this app

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you can't access the App, email us and we will delete your account and data manually within 30 days.

Please send your request from the email address you used to sign in, so we can verify it's you, to:

ecofamilyflow@gmail.com

For more detail on what we collect and why, see our Privacy Policy.
