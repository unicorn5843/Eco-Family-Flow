📘 Your Guide

🤱Milk – Track breastfeeding / pumping sessions. Shows a supply vs demand chart.
🍼Formula – Log bottle feeds (ml) with 7‑day average.
🧻Nappy – Record wet, dirty, or both. Graphs trends separately.
❤️Skin/Tummy – Log skin‑to‑skin and tummy time with 7‑day averages.
💊Meds – Log medications. Live timer shows time since last dose. Set native reminders.
🏥Hospital – Notes from any medical visit.
📈Trending – 7-day patterns, monthly charts, forecasts, and PDF report export.
🛒Deals – Compare prices per nappy/wipe/formula across brands.
❄️Stash – Manually track frozen breastmilk inventory.
📅Appts – Schedule, edit and manage appointments. Past appointments are archived for reference.
💰Savings – Quick links to money‑saving offers.
📋Private Notes – Stored only on this device, never synced.
📍Emergency – One‑tap search for nearby pharmacies, A&E, or GPs.
