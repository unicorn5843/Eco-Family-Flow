Privacy Policy for Eco Family Flow

Last updated: 5 August 2026

Eco Family Flow (the "App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, store, and share your personal data when you use the App.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you sign in with Google, we receive your email address and unique user ID. We do not store your password.
  • Data You Enter: All logs you create (milk, formula, nappy, medications, hospital notes, appointments, etc.) are stored in your Firebase account and associated with your email and the selected child profile.
  • Family Data: If you create or join a family, we store the family name, invite code, and members' email addresses to enable sharing.
  • Stash Balance: The frozen milk stash amount you manually update is stored in your Firebase account.
  • Private Notes: These are stored only on your device’s local storage and are never synced to our servers.
  • Location Data: When you use the Emergency feature ("Find Nearby"), we request your device’s location only to provide relevant search results. Location data is never stored or transmitted to our servers.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide the core functionality of the App (tracking, sharing with family, analytics, reminders).
  • To send optional medication reminders (if you allow notifications).
  • To improve the App based on aggregated usage patterns (no personal data is used for this).

3. Data Storage and Security

  • All user‑entered data is stored in Google Firebase (Firestore and Authentication), which uses strong encryption both in transit and at rest.
  • Your data is associated with your Google account and is accessible only to you and the family members you have explicitly invited.
  • Private notes remain solely on your device and are not backed up anywhere else.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

  • You can delete any individual log entry directly from the App.
  • You can delete your entire account and all associated data via the "Delete Account & Data" button in the Account page. This permanently removes your logs, stash balance, family membership, and sign-in record from Firebase.
  • If you uninstall the App, your data remains in Firebase until you choose to delete it.
  • If you cannot access the App, you can request account and data deletion at our account deletion page, or by emailing ecofamilyflow@gmail.com.

5. Third‑Party Services

  • Google Firebase: Provides authentication, database, and hosting services. Firebase Privacy Policy.
  • Google Sign‑In: Uses Google’s OAuth 2.0; you can review Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • Chart.js and other CDN libraries are loaded client‑side and do not collect data.

6. Children’s Privacy

The App is intended for use by parents and guardians to track their children's health information. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. All data entered is provided by adults.

7. Your Rights

  • Access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time through the App.
  • Withdraw consent for data processing (by deleting your account).
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.

9. Contact

For any privacy questions or requests, please contact us at: ecofamilyflow@gmail.com.
